#+TITLE: Letters from the Lean model of graded transpose
#+DATE: 2026-09-08
#+CATEGORY: lean-transpose

Seventeen letters, written across one project: a Lean 4 model of the
grading design behind P3200 and beman.transpose, built one law at a time
to find out exactly which property of a grade each law actually needs.

Reading note: each letter was written the day its proof went green, in
the order below. Nothing was rewritten to look more decided in hindsight
than it was at the time; where a later letter corrects an earlier one,
the earlier letter says so in place rather than being quietly edited.
